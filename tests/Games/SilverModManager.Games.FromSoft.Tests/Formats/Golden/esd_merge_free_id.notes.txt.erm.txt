Produced once with erm (read-only), working directory outside both repos. The base, other and vanilla machines are
the ones EsdMergeGoldenCases builds for "free_id", built there and here with the same ermlib.formats.esd records:
  merged, notes = esdmerge.merge(base, other, vanilla)
This file holds the sentences esdmerge.describe prints for the notes of that merge, one per line (empty when the merge has none).
Compared byte-for-byte (the machine) or line-for-line (the notes).
